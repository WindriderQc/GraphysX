This is a low Poly Cobra model that can be used for most 
any application.

When you import this model into your 3D program, you'll 
need to re-weld most of the Vertex's for the smoothing 
groups to work correctly. Then Apply the Textures.

There are multiple colors included; Black, Gray, Blue, Green, Orange, and Red.
