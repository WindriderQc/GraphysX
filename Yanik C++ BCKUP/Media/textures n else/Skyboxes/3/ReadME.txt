How do I use this *Envirobox*?

Upload all the zipped .bmps and the .jpgs to your textures folder on your op.
Upload the zipped .rwx file to your models folder on your op.

In your AW browser's menu (at the top) go to:
	Options / World / Features
	Click the + sign for Sky
	Under Skybox put in the name of the .rwx (Ex: lnhenv_6sa)
		The .rwx part is not needed here, just the file name itself
	Click OK and wait for the Envirobox to load into your world

Once the envirobox, it's textures and masks, load into your world
	you are ready to start playing with the aw sky colors 
	Again in the browser's menu go to: 
	Options / World / Features
	Click the + sign for Sky
	Now you will work on changing sky colors 
	(Top, North, East, South, West and Bottom).
	Examples for what I've used for Envirobox1 sky colors are: 
	Top: Red=48; Green=91; Blue=228
	N/S/W/E are all: Red=128; Green=172; Blue=228
	Bottom: Red=166; Green=217; Blue=184 (matches my water color)
		You can make these colors anything you like btw!

Next do your cloud layers according to your liking!

Voila ... you have an Envirobox! Enjoy!!!

NOTES: There are NO top or bottom on these, they are not needed and their 
absence will actually allow your envirobox to load faster than if they were 
included. This set includes THREE different boxes so you can use them for 
summer (& spring) OR fall (& spring) OR winter. I left a gap in both
East and West directions so you can now use a sun or moon jpg if you
choose to have a bot control your weather/atmosphere! Aeon world is
